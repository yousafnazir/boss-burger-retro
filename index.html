About us

The original
Boss burger story

“When Christian’s behind the grill, every bite is pure flavor.”

Christian Cuellar is a self-taught cook who sharpened his craft on the DFW food-truck circuit. Now at 1905 East Arkansas Lane, he grinds the beef fresh, builds the sauces in-house, and seasons every burger to order — no freezers, no shortcuts.

Why Boss?

Why choose us

  1. 01

    Fast, easy ordering

    Drive through one of our two lanes or walk up to the window. Hot food, no apps required — or order ahead and skip the line.
